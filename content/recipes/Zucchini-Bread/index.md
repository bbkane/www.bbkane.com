# Ingredients

- 2 cups oatmeal, ground down (we did it in a blender)
- 2-3 cups grated zucchini
- 2 "eggs" (we used chia seed eggs)
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup dark chocolate chips
- cinnamon to taste
- honey to taste (or other sweetener)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 cup walnuts
- 1 cup Almond milk (or until a soft batter forms)
- 1-2 tbsp fat (avocado oil, olive oil, benecol)

Sometimes we add bananas or peanut butter

# Instructions

Mix in a big bowl

Bake at 350 degrees Farenheit for 50-60 min
